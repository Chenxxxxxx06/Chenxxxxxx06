# Profile artwork and icons

The pixel cottage banner, observatory illustration, and valley footer were generated for this profile using OpenAI's built-in image generation tool, based on the owner's approved concept. These are original generated scenes, not assets extracted from Stardew Valley.

The technology SVG strips use icons from [Skill Icons](https://github.com/tandpfun/skill-icons), retrieved on 2026-10-22. Their MIT license is reproduced in [SKILL-ICONS-LICENSE.txt](SKILL-ICONS-LICENSE.txt). Product names and logos identify their respective tools. Original icons are grouped into local SVG strips so rendering does not depend on a third-party badge endpoint.

The AI tool badges and availability badge are original SVG UI graphics. Their pixel terminal/chat/sprout symbols are generic pictograms, not official product logos.

The Argus description is summarized from [lbx154/Argus](https://github.com/lbx154/Argus/blob/main/README.md) and its [Chinese README](https://github.com/lbx154/Argus/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md), retrieved on 2026-10-22. The featured link follows the owner's requested development-preview repository; no authorship or benchmark claim is implied.
